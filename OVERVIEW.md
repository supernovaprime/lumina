### OVERVIEW 1
CarWash is a web and mobile application designed to provide customers with a convenient way to find and access car-washing services. The application connects customers with car-wash businesses, allowing customers to view available services, compare prices, select their vehicle type, check available time slots, and book appointments without having to visit or contact the car-wash business physically.
The application will provide different user roles, primarily customers, car-wash businesses, and administrators.
Customer Features
Customers will be able to create and manage their accounts, browse available car-wash businesses, view the services they offer, and see the prices for each service. Customers can select their vehicle type, choose a preferred car-washing package, select an available date and time, and make a booking.
Customers will also have access to a personal dashboard where they can view upcoming and previous bookings, manage their profile, view their booking history, and manage their vehicles.
The application can also provide notifications to remind customers about upcoming appointments and provide updates concerning their bookings.
Car-Wash Business Features
Car-wash businesses will have their own dashboard through which they can manage their business information, services, prices, operating hours, and availability.
Businesses will be able to view and manage customer bookings, helping them organize their daily schedules and avoid conflicts between appointments. They can also view relevant information about their customers and monitor their business activity.
Administrator Features
Administrators will oversee the overall operation of the platform. They will be able to manage customers, car-wash businesses, services, bookings, and other important aspects of the system.
Additional Features
The application can be expanded to include online payments, ratings and reviews, location-based search, favorites, booking history, and business analytics.
Customers could search for nearby car washes, compare businesses based on prices and ratings, and leave reviews after receiving a service. Businesses could also receive useful information about their bookings and revenue to help them understand their performance.
Overall Goal
The main goal of CarWash is to make car-washing services more convenient, organized, accessible, and efficient for both customers and service providers.
Instead of customers having to physically visit different car washes or contact them individually to find prices and availability, CarWash brings these services together in one platform where customers can discover, compare, and book services, while businesses can efficiently manage their operations.


### OVERVIEW 2
CarWash Application Overview
CarWash is a web and mobile service platform designed to connect customers with car-washing businesses and provide a complete digital solution for discovering, booking, managing, and receiving car-washing services.
The application will allow customers to find car-wash businesses, view their services and prices, select their vehicle type, compare available options, check appointment availability, book a preferred date and time, make payments, receive notifications, and manage their service history.
At the same time, car-wash businesses will have access to management tools that allow them to manage their services, pricing, schedules, bookings, customers, and business performance.
Customer Experience
Customers will be able to create accounts and maintain personal profiles containing information about themselves and their vehicles. They can browse available car-wash businesses and view important information such as services, prices, operating hours, ratings, reviews, location, and availability.
Customers can select their vehicle, choose a service package, select a convenient date and time, review their booking, and make an online payment where supported.
The application can provide booking confirmations, reminders, and other notifications to keep customers informed about their appointments.
Customers will also be able to view upcoming and previous bookings, manage multiple vehicles, save favorite car-wash businesses, provide ratings and reviews, and view their payment and service history.
Car-Wash Business Management
Car-wash businesses will have dedicated dashboards for managing their operations.
Businesses can create and update their services, prices, descriptions, estimated service durations, operating hours, and available appointment slots. They can view incoming bookings, manage appointments, access customer information relevant to their services, and monitor their business activity.
The platform can also provide business analytics, allowing businesses to monitor bookings, revenue, popular services, customer activity, and other useful performance indicators.
Booking and Payment System
The application will provide a structured booking process in which customers select a vehicle, service, date, and available time slot.
The system will prevent conflicting appointments by managing the availability of each car-wash business.
Online payment functionality can allow customers to pay for services through the application and receive payment confirmations and transaction records.
Location and Discovery
CarWash can include location-based features that allow customers to discover car-wash businesses near them.
Customers can search for nearby businesses and compare them using factors such as distance, prices, ratings, available services, and appointment availability. Map and navigation features can also help customers locate the selected car-wash business.
Reviews and Customer Engagement
After completing a service, customers can rate and review their experience. These ratings can help other customers make informed decisions while also allowing businesses to monitor customer satisfaction.
The platform can further introduce loyalty programs, rewards, promotions, discounts, and subscription packages to encourage customers to continue using the service.
Advanced and Future Features
As the platform develops, CarWash can introduce additional services and intelligent features.
Potential future features include:
Loyalty and reward programs
Promotional offers and discounts
Subscription-based car-washing packages
Pickup and drop-off services
Real-time booking and service status
Advanced business analytics
Personalized service recommendations
Multiple vehicles under one customer account
Automated notifications and reminders
Customer service and complaint management
These features would allow CarWash to evolve from a simple appointment-booking application into a broader digital car-care service platform.
Administrator Management

Administrators will manage the overall platform and ensure that the system operates properly.
The administrator dashboard can provide management of customers, businesses, bookings, services, payments, reviews, complaints, promotions, and platform activity.
Administrators can also access statistics and reports that provide an overview of the platform's performance.
Overall Vision
The overall vision of CarWash is to create a convenient digital ecosystem where customers can discover, compare, book, pay for, and manage car-washing services, while businesses receive the tools necessary to manage and grow their operations.
The platform will begin with the essential features required for customers and businesses to interact effectively, while providing a foundation that can support more advanced services such as payments, location-based discovery, loyalty programs, subscriptions, pickup and drop-off services, and intelligent recommendations.
Ultimately, CarWash aims to make accessing and managing car-washing services faster, easier, more transparent, and more convenient for both customers and service providers.
